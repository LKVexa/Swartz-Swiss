Refresh and numerical time

Display redraws are paced by the browser's animation clock and the monitor. Workers advance numerical equations independently, with bounded queues and measured throughput. Faster refresh does not enlarge a CFL-limited physical time step, turn a static reference into an evolution, or give a stationary Kerr solver additional physical dynamics. Current rates are shown in each instrument. A solver can produce fewer new states per second than the display draws.

Included repositories

All original JA21 files and the exact supplied archives are retained in the portable package. Deep original reference paths are mapped through the preserved provenance index. Active model code and data remain in GFM/ and GFM/experiments/phase/. Source data, licenses, audits, old reports and raw observations accompany the instruments. Historical reports retain their original scope and dates.
