PHASE, MATTER AND ELECTROMAGNETIC FIELD EXPERIMENT
Prepared 2026-10-11

The new texture layer evolves a local plasma experiment, a neutral complex
field and a separate iron enthalpy laboratory. Its displayed colors derive
from computed fields. Placing that patch beside the black-hole illustration
does not make it a solution for matter near a Kerr horizon.

The imported EHT observations test separate circular brightness surrogates.
Both fail the held-out 2013 comparison. No empirical validation of the plasma,
complex-phase law, iron parcel, magnetic texture or aperture cover follows.
See observation-validation.txt and observation-sources.json for the actual
data, uncertainties, frozen protocol, predictions and independent results.

ACTUAL COMPUTED MODELS

1. Plasma and electromagnetic fields

backend/phase-mhd.js evolves compressible, nonrelativistic MHD on a square
periodic Cartesian grid of 16, 32 or 64 cells per side. The fixed ideal-gas
closure is gamma=5/3 and mean molecular weight=0.5. In normalized units:
  E=p/(gamma-1)+rho*|v|^2/2+|B|^2/2; T=p/rho.
  rho_t+div(rho*v)=0.
  (rho*v)_t+div[rho*v*v+(p+|B|^2/2)I-B*B-tau]=0.
  E_t+div[(E+p+|B|^2/2)v-(v.B)B-tau.v-kappa*grad(T)
           +eta*(J cross B)]=0.
  B_t=-curl(Eelectric); Eelectric=-v cross B+eta*J; J=curl(B).
The 2D fields have in-plane velocity and magnetic components and a normal
current/electric component. A passive concentration follows advection and
diffusion; it is not an independently evolved solid material.

The method uses first-order Rusanov finite-volume spatial fluxes, face magnetic
fields, a shared corner electric field for constrained transport and SSP RK2
time integration. CFL, diffusion and reaction limits control the step size.
Rejected positivity steps reduce the time step; numerical failures are
reported without silently applying pressure/density floors. Artificial
diffusion from the discretization remains and requires refinement checks.

The default normalization is T0=20000 K, n0=1e15 nuclei/m^3 and t0=1 s:
  rho0=mp*n0; v0=sqrt(kB*T0/(0.5*mp)); L0=v0*t0;
  B0=v0*sqrt(mu0*rho0).
Temperature, velocity, magnetic/electric field and current have explicit
conversion scales in each export. The periodic domain length is expressed
in L0. Normalized resistivity, viscosity and conduction are declared model
parameters; they are not measured M87 transport coefficients.

This closure omits relativity, Kerr gravitational forces, radiation,
collisionless particle distributions, anisotropic conduction, ionization
energy and a variable-ionization equation of state. Hydrogen ground-state
LTE Saha ionization is a reference diagnostic only. Its fractions do not
change the fixed MHD equation of state. LTE has not been established for this
patch or for the optically thin astronomical plasma seen by EHT.

2. Complex phase, amplitude and vortices

The model evolves a neutral phenomenological complex Ginzburg-Landau field:
  psi_t+v.grad(psi)=D*(1+i*c1)*laplacian(psi)
      +[mu0-cT*(T-1)+crho*(rho-1)-cB*|B|^2
        -g*|psi|^2-i*omega*|psi|^2]*psi.
Plasma influences psi through advection and the declared growth terms. Psi
does not exert a force on the plasma. The coefficients are illustrative,
not inferred from EHT observations or microscopic matter experiments.

Amplitude is abs(psi); phase is arg(psi). Four-corner wrapped phase changes
give a plaquette winding indicator. Low-amplitude or nearly pi edge samples
are flagged unresolved; locations are cell-plaquette centers, not fitted
subcell vortex cores. Undefined phase is stored as zero solely for finite
JSON transport, with a separate phaseDefined flag. This is not a charged
wavefunction, quantum probability, condensate or observed VLBI phase.

Magnetic phase arg(Bx+i*By) separately describes in-plane field direction.
Its zeros and windings do not imply flux quantization or superconductivity.
The displayed density, temperature and magnetic textures are computed field
maps; visual resemblance to a telescope image is not a likelihood test.

3. Solid, liquid, vapor and first-ionized iron diagnostic

A separate grid evolves iron specific enthalpy, with physical length default
0.1 m, fixed density 7874 kg/m^3 and reference pressure 1 bar. It solves:
  h_t=(k/rhoFe)*laplacian(Tiron)+Qheat
          +Cbath*(Tplasma-Tiron),
with conversion from local simulation time to seconds. This is a separate
isobaric parcel model, not the MHD plasma equation of state or a cold cover.

Declared approximations are melting at 1811 K, boiling at 3134 K, constant
heat capacities 450/820/372.2 J/(kg K), fusion enthalpy 13810/0.055845 J/kg
and vaporization enthalpy 340000/0.055845 J/kg. Finite latent-enthalpy intervals
hold temperature at the transition while changing the phase fraction.
These rounded constants are not a full NIST-JANAF thermodynamic fit or an
uncertainty-aware pressure-dependent phase diagram. NIST data independently
support the iron melting/fusion scale and provide richer temperature-dependent
heat capacities that this approximation does not implement.

The vapor is partitioned into neutral and first-ionized diagnostic fractions
using an LTE ground-state Saha reference at a declared nuclei density. Solid,
liquid, neutral vapor and first-ionized fractions sum to one. Multiple
ionization, latent ionization heat, expansion, stresses, elastic dynamics,
magnetic material properties and pressure evolution are omitted.

GFM REPRESENTATION AND OBSERVABLES

Nine selected spatial samples of psi are indexed by GF(9) and transformed in
a complex finite-field Fourier basis. An inverse check tests representation
recovery of the original complex samples. The implemented session performs
no unit normalization: input and transform sample energies are exported and
original amplitudes are retained. This representation does not alter the MHD
equations or provide a physical coupling to the aperture hypothesis.

The previously verified neutral Kerr null trajectories remain a separate
vacuum exterior calculation. The aperture/aggie cover rule remains a declared
hypothesis. Neither calculation receives a self-consistent stress-energy
tensor from this local patch. No GRMHD, accretion model, polarized ray transfer
or astronomical matter-to-visibility forward model is implemented here.

The EHT amplitude release contains neither calibrated polarimetry nor complex
visibility phases. The published EHT polarization analyses use a richer
observable/model chain. Their inferred magnetic/temperature ranges are model
dependent and are not direct local-field targets used to tune this experiment.

Runtime criteria are relative global mass and total MHD energy errors below
1e-8, normalized staggered div(B) below 1e-10, finite fields and positive
density, pressure and material temperature. Passing these checks establishes
numerical consistency for a run, not accuracy of its dynamics or empirical
validation. Analytic limiting cases and spatial/time refinement are needed
in addition to conserved totals. Record source hashes, parameters, boundary
conditions, accepted/rejected steps and the measured errors with each result.

The final phase-specific suite passes 12 tests. Evidence is in
validation/phase-numerical-results.json and phase-verification-summary.txt.
The checked backend/public engine bytes have SHA256:
  1a834a359572281279868c4dd29403b7a8dd13d2f386ccc9f214917048e30373
Independent reconstruction of the exported conserved and staggered arrays
measured relative mass error <=8.89e-16, relative total-energy error <=1.56e-15
and maximum staggered magnetic divergence <=5.66e-15. The checks do not rely
solely on the runtime pass flag.

Spatial refinement over 16/32/64 cells gives approximately first-order
convergence: scalar advection orders 0.943/0.974, scalar diffusion 0.949/0.978
and small magnetic-mode decay 0.965/0.986. Uniform complex reaction has
temporal orders 1.997/1.998. Numerical diffusion is substantial: at 64 cells,
eta=0.05 and time=0.4, the magnetic-mode amplitude ratio is 0.967879 rather
than the continuum 0.980199. Conservation does not erase that discrepancy.

Independent iron heating/enthalpy balance differs by at most 4.66e-9 J/kg;
periodic conduction mean enthalpy differs by 5.82e-11 J/kg. The known complex
test field has two positive and two negative winding charges, net zero and
location agreement within the plaquette-resolution bound. Invalid inputs,
failed steps, trusted-callback reset rollback and deterministic replay are
covered. These smooth-mode and parcel tests are not a turbulent, high-Mach,
kinetic-plasma or empirical calibration suite. Estimated resolved Ohmic and
viscous heating integrals are not an exact entropy/thermal-energy balance.

This is a bounded research demonstration. The project does not claim NASA
certification, observational validation of a novel phase-matter law or a
physically established liquid/solid surface near a black hole.
