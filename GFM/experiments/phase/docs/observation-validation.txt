Prepared 2026-10-11

The data pipeline and independent arithmetic checks passed. Both simple
stationary brightness surrogates failed the untouched 2013 observation check.
This is an implemented empirical comparison with a reported failure, not an
accepted validation of the phase field, matter model, magnetic texture or MHD.

FIXED TRAINING/HOLDOUT PROTOCOL

data/observations/protocol.json was fixed before the first project fit. Source
headers and some example rows had already been inspected, so this is not a
blinded or externally registered preregistration.

Training: 2011+2012. Holdout: 2013. Select projected baseline length >=0.2 Glambda
on both splits; this excludes intra-site total-flux rows from a compact-only
surrogate. There are 173 training rows, 146 holdout rows and 167 excluded rows.
Excluded measurements remain in the preserved source and parsed dataset.

Two circular brightness models are compared using the Fourier visibility
relation. With rho=sqrt(u^2+v^2), angular scales in radians and compact flux F:
  Thin emission ring: amplitude=F*abs(J0(pi*diameter*rho)).
  Circular Gaussian: amplitude=F*exp[-(pi*FWHM*rho)^2/(4*ln(2))].
These are geometric emission surrogates. The angular emission-ring diameter
is not equated with the geometric photon-capture shadow diameter.

At each scale on a 0..120 microarcsecond grid with 0.1 microarcsecond spacing,
fit a single nonnegative compact flux (0..10 Jy) analytically by minimizing
sum[(observed-predicted)/sigma]^2 using training rows only. Both years share
that flux. Diameter/FWHM, flux, selection, error model and acceptance thresholds
stay fixed when predicting 2013. No holdout-specific gain, flux or error-floor
adjustment is made.

The project diagnostic requires normalized residual RMS <=2 and at least 95%
of rows within 3 publisher RMS errors, on both training and holdout. These are
declared diagnostic thresholds, not an astrophysical significance test.

Gaussian    38.2 microas FWHM  0.828223 Jy    1.2312        13.5309      4.1096%

Both training diagnostics pass; both unchanged holdout predictions fail.
The ring's slightly better training residual does not establish a held-out
success or uniquely identify a ring in the sparse early-EHT observations.
public/observation-report.json and validation/observation-fit-report.json
retain the entire training-grid profile and all selected observed/predicted
values and residuals. A formal delta-chi-square=1 grid interval is conditional
on the diagonal-error surrogate and is not accepted as a physical confidence
interval when the hypothesis/calibration assumptions fail.

UNCERTAINTY AND INTERPRETATION

Publisher RMS errors are treated as independent Gaussian amplitude errors.
Station gain correlations, calibration covariance and non-Gaussian low-SNR
amplitude likelihoods are omitted. A time-independent circular morphology
with one shared flux also omits brightness asymmetry, extended emission and
intrinsic variability. The observed discrepancy does not by itself identify
which omission is responsible. It is not evidence against Kerr gravity.
No formal p-value is reported from this incomplete likelihood.

This amplitude-only release contains no calibrated polarization or complex
visibility phases. The phase of a Fourier coefficient is not a material
order-parameter phase. The data do not separately estimate local density,
temperature, magnetic field, solid/liquid/gas/plasma state or vortices.
GR radiative transfer, particle distributions and a specified observer model
would be needed to connect simulated matter/fields to these measurements.
The present brightness surrogate has no such connection.

Independent mass/distance measurements can provide an additional angular
shadow-scale sanity check without using EHT-inferred mass as an input. Stellar
and gas dynamical masses disagree and carry model systematics. Any future such
check must preserve each measurement's M/D convention and distinguish its
predicted geometric shadow from the observed emission ring. This scalar check
is not used to replace the real baseline holdout comparison above.

INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

validation/validate-observations.py imports no production module. It reparses
all 486 publisher rows, checks hashes and reproduces both 1201-point training
grids. It evaluates J0 by periodic angular quadrature (NIST DLMF 10.9.1),
independently of the production power series. Every training and holdout
prediction/residual was checked. Maximum prediction disagreement was
3.331e-16 Jy. Altered holdout predictions and false acceptance labels were
deliberately rejected. validation/observation-independent-results.json records
checker, data, protocol, model and report hashes.

Calculation verificationPassed=true and observationComparisonPassed=false
are different results and remain separate in machine-readable evidence.
The comparison adds an empirical test and an explicit model failure. It does
not validate a new complex-phase matter law, GRMHD solver, iron laboratory,
black-hole cover or physical passage. No NASA certification is claimed.
