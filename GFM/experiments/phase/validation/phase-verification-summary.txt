API and robustness:
Invalid parameter objects, unknown/symbol keys, nonfinite/out-of-range values,
accessor properties and invalid time intervals preserve the previous frame.
Accessors are rejected without being invoked. Returned frames cannot modify the
model. Identical step sequences, reset and valid parameter-reset experiments
replay deterministically. Failed positivity steps restore physical fields and
elapsed/accepted-step ledgers while exposing an error/rejection diagnostic.

A real trusted-callback reset defect was found and fixed: initialization could
previously replace parameters and partially replace state before a callback
failure. setParameters/reset now restore the complete previous experiment on
callback exceptions or invalid sampled states. Regression tests cover both.

Equation audit:
E_z=v_y B_x-v_x B_y+eta J_z with B_x,t=-d_y E_z and B_y,t=d_x E_z has the
Faraday/Ohm damping signs. The shared corner curl preserves discrete divergence.
The resistive conservative energy flux has x=-eta J_z B_y and y=eta J_z B_x,
the correct E cross B signs. Viscous energy flux is -tau dot v and thermal flux
is -k grad(p/rho). Periodic finite-volume sums telescope. Complex diffusion is
D(1+i beta)laplacian(psi), and nonlinear frequency is -i omega|psi|^2 psi.
The separate iron periodic enthalpy balance tracks heating/bath sources without
subtracting them from MHD energy; it is a separate laboratory, not two-way matter
coupling.

Material limitations:
First-order Rusanov/CT spatial diffusion is visible even at n=64 and can exceed
the declared physical diffusion. RK2 temporal order does not establish second-
order spatial accuracy. Global energy conservation and divB preservation do not
establish an accurate local entropy or dissipative heating budget. Resolved
Ohmic/viscous heating integrals are estimates, not exact physical heating totals.

The solver is a local periodic, nonrelativistic 2D ideal-gas MHD model with fixed
gamma=5/3 and mean molecular weight 0.5. It has no Kerr sources, radiation,
accretion, horizon or solid-cover mechanics. Saha ionization is a diagnostic
LTE reference and does not alter the fixed EOS. The neutral complex field has
one-way phenomenological couplings; magnetic direction/winding does not imply
flux quantization or a quantum condensate. Iron uses declared constant heat
capacities, transition temperatures and latent heats; it has no pressure-
dependent EOS, expansion or solid mechanics. These are numerical verification
results, not empirical or astronomical validation.
