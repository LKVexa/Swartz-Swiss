GFM PULSE TRANSPORT AND WORMHOLE-STYLE DISPLAY - v2.1.0

The pulse is the live computed electromagnetic field. No independent
particle animation or invented propagation speed is used.

TRANSPORT
Instantaneous net Poynting flux: Sr = Ephi Hz, Sz = -Ephi Hr, in W/m2.
Forward (+z) arrows point toward the receiving cover. Returning (-z)
arrows include reflected and reactive/interference contributions. Their
length uses a fixed declared |S| reference; changing a display scale does
not change the field. A backward arrow alone is not a reflection ratio.

The optional upstream vacuum port decomposes collocated samples into
E+ = (Ephi-Z0 Hr)/2 and E- = (Ephi+Z0 Hr)/2, I+/- = E+/- squared / Z0.
I+ - I- = -Ephi Hr is an algebraic identity. These are local vacuum
characteristic channels, not a unique global incident/reflected split.
The sampled annuli avoid plasma, cover, sponge and the source neighborhood;
the axial Gaussian source tail is suppressed beyond eight widths. If no
qualifying row exists, the port is unavailable, with no fabricated power.

ABSORPTION AND ENERGY
Plasma collision power density: qnu = nu Jp squared / alpha, where alpha
already includes the plasma fill fraction. Cover Ohmic power density:
qc = sigma_full_cell Ephi squared; conductivity already includes cover fill.
The display can switch between instantaneous absorbed power, accumulated
collision/Ohmic deposition, and plasma temperature increase.

The destination ledger uses the solver's raw staggered field energy:
initial energy + signed source work = field + reversible plasma drift
+ plasma thermal change + cover thermal change + artificial sponge loss
- numerical residual.
The reported residual is accounted minus available energy.
Accumulated deposition is shown separately from current thermal storage.
It is not added to stored thermal energy again. Thermal contact transfers
energy internally, subtracting from one medium and adding to the other.
Artificial sponge loss is distinct from physical plasma/cover absorption.
PEC domain boundaries have zero physical escaped energy in this model.

WORMHOLE-STYLE SHAPE
The display radially flares the solved cylinder's two mouths and narrows
its visual waist. The cylindrical equations, material cells, source,
propagation and energy ledger remain unchanged. All layers share this
mapping; vector arrows use its Jacobian. Scalars remain in physical SI
units. The solved cylindrical shape is available for comparison.
This appearance is not a solved GR metric, causal throat, event horizon,
or demonstration of traversability. The neutral CGLE reference and GR
reference calculators remain separate studies.
