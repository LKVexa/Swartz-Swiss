DEFINITIONS AND SCOPE

A black hole is a region unable to send a causal signal to selected exterior
future null infinity; an event horizon is a global causal boundary. A
traversable wormhole requires a specified spacetime with causal passage,
boundary conditions, regularity and traveler-specific constraints. The
aperture/aggie is the user's local opening/cover hypothesis. A material hole,
a marginal sphere or exterior observational similarity proves none of these.

This instrument evolves local SI fields on an r-z grid. The rendered volume
is an m=0 axisymmetric reconstruction; no azimuthal perturbations are evolved.
The older neutral dimensionless CGLE snapshot remains a separate reference.
It is not a Maxwell field, temperature, charge wavefunction or spacetime.

ACTIVE ELECTROMAGNETIC AND PLASMA EQUATIONS

E=E_phi e_phi; H=H_r e_r+H_z e_z; d/dphi=0.
epsilon0 E_phi,t = H_r,z - H_z,r - J_p - sigma_eff E_phi + F_phi

E is V/m, H A/m, B=mu0 H tesla, J A/m^2. The regular prescribed Gaussian
ring current is O(r) at the axis. It is forcing, not a specified incident
electric amplitude. E_phi and H_r are O(r); H_z is finite on the axis.
The retained azimuthally uniform E and J have zero divergence with neutral
initial charge. Other charge modes require more field components.

J_p,t+nu J_p=alpha E_phi
alpha=f_p n_e e^2/m_e=f_p epsilon0 omega_p^2
omega_p^2=n_e e^2/(epsilon0 m_e)
K_p=J_p^2/(2alpha); q_nu=nu J_p^2/alpha>=0
E_phi J_p=K_p,t+q_nu.

Intrinsic n_e is per plasma volume; J_p is per full computational-cell
volume. Fixed fractions obey f_p+f_c<=1. At alpha=0, J_p=K_p=q_nu=0;
no artificial density floor is used. Plasma current is not counted again
as an equivalent Ohmic conductivity. At nu=0 its work is reversible drift
storage, not heat. Instantaneous E*J can be negative while q_nu remains
nonnegative. For frozen harmonic coefficients with exp(-i omega t),
sigma_p=alpha/(nu-i omega); temporal current governs actual finite pulses.

Fixed stationary ions, fixed density and geometry, unmagnetized electrons:
there is no imposed B0/Hall bias, bulk rotation, advection or ionization.
These omissions are deliberate limits of the retained polarization.

The single plasma temperature assumes electron/ion equilibration. Thermal
contact acts only in mixed cells with both media, transfers equal/opposite
energy and defaults to zero. It is a phenomenological volumetric contact,
not a resolved measured surface conductance. Spatial thermal conduction is
absent. Specific enthalpy is J/kg, c_p J/(kg K), latent heat L_m J/kg.

Material parameters are declared synthetic coefficients, not measurements
of an aggie, iron, aluminum or an astrophysical plasma. The solid/liquid
interval is a mushy regularization, not a pure-material equilibrium plateau.
Memoryless Ohmic cover response is a constitutive approximation; predictive
metal dispersion requires calibrated Drude/Lorentz coefficients.

Geometry is independent: r_clear=(R_c-wallThickness)*sqrt(1-C_g).
Melting does not remove cover mass or open the mouth. That would require
mechanics/ablation/vaporization. Parameter changes reset the fields, history
and energy ledger together. The warm-start cover preset starts inside the
mushy interval; its initial melting state is not caused by the pulse.

ENERGY, FLUX AND NUMERICS

u_EM=(epsilon0 E^2+mu0 H^2)/2; S=E cross H.
S_r=E_phi H_z; S_z=-E_phi H_r [W/m^2].
The receiving mouth is at the smaller z end, outward normal -e_z. Positive
S_z is net inward flux. The signed plane integral is not incident/reflected
mode separation. Positive-only intake can count recrossings and is not
absorptance; amplitude alone is not total energy in standing/dispersive waves.

Delta(U_EM+K_p+U_thermal,plasma+U_thermal,cover)+Q_sponge=W_source
up to a numerical residual, for this closed PEC domain. Internal contact
cancels. Cumulative collision/Joule heat is exported but not added again
when thermal stored energy is in the ledger. Tiny temperature increments
are retained independently of rounded absolute-temperature values.

Outer radial/axial boundaries are reflecting PEC. Physical boundary escape
is zero. The optional electric sponge is a separate artificial sink, not
a PML, radiation, or material absorption; its reflection is not quantified.

Finite-volume annular cells have volume pi(r_out^2-r_in^2) dz. H occupies
dual faces. Weighted-adjoint curls and implicit midpoint preserve the
field/drift quadratic work identity. Material coefficients are frozen for
a field substep and refreshed from updated enthalpy. This is not a
monolithic nonlinear moving-interface solve. Time steps must resolve
propagation, carrier/envelope, plasma, collision and Ohmic relaxation.
A-stability or a closed ledger does not guarantee accuracy for stiffness.
Geometric cell fractions homogenize interfaces and do not resolve skin depth.
