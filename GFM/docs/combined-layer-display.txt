GFM COMBINED 3D DISPLAY CONTRACT - v2.0.2

One snapshot supplies the electromagnetic, plasma and cover quantities.
The two crossing axial slices reconstruct circumferentially uniform
scalar fields from the existing r-z cells. They are display samples of
the m=0 model, not a new general three-dimensional field calculation.

WAVE SLICE
Hue = relativeCarrierPhaseRad; brightness = envelopeAmplitudeVPerM on a
fixed 0-100 V/m peak-amplitude reference. carrierPhaseValidMask excludes
unresolved phase. The upstream TOTAL-field reference includes reflections.
The causal Hann phase window has a group delay; its analysis time is
displayed independently of the instantaneous snapshot time. At a 2 ns
snapshot the default phase analysis is centred at approximately 1.8333 ns.

PLASMA SLICE
Color = plasmaTemperatureRiseK on a fixed 0-0.002 K range. A cell requires
positive plasma fill and positive full-cell heat capacity. Heating is
relative to the initial plasma temperature. Zero-density plasma has no
temperature-rise sample. Color saturation is counted in the legend.

AGGIE SURFACE
Color = instantaneous joulePowerDensityWPerM3 = fc*sigma_c*Ephi^2,
on a fixed 0-20 W/m3 range, adjustable through the cover scale upper input.
The power density is normalized to the full numerical cell volume.
Analytic stationary material surfaces sample actual intersected material
cells. Cover temperature and liquid fraction remain separate reported
quantities, rather than being inferred from deposition color.
At covered 2 ns, maximum power density is about 17.60 W/m3 and maximum
plasma heating is about 0.001242 K. Cover temperature rise is only about
2.90e-14 K and liquid fraction remains zero. The pulse does not melt it.

GEOMETRY AND OVERLAP
All layers use the same metre-to-display coordinate transform. Neither
electric amplitude nor thermal/liquid state displaces the material.
Plasma and material masks can overlap in homogenized fractional cells;
both responses remain represented there. Cover fraction prescribes the
geometric clear mouth. Melting does not remove or open the cover.
Cutaway and visibility controls affect rendering only. They do not remove
matter, suppress a physical current, change source forcing or alter energy.
Transparency is a display property, not calculated optical depth.

The numerical equations, phase history and audit registry are unchanged.
General-relativistic reference calculators and the independent neutral
CGLE phase texture remain separate from this local Maxwell calculation.
