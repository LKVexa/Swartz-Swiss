GFM CYLINDRICAL PULSE SIMULATOR — SCIENTIFIC SPECIFICATION
Independent equation and validation review, 2026-10-11

1. WHAT THE TWO DISPLAYS MEAN

The phase-texture cylinder and the electromagnetic pulse experiment are separate
mathematical objects. The phase cylinder embeds the earlier periodic complex
field on a cylindrical surface. The electromagnetic experiment evolves a new
axisymmetric Maxwell field in a volume. No conversion of the earlier planar
Ginzburg–Landau equations into volumetric Maxwell equations is claimed.

For a planar periodic source field psi(x,y), the display mapping is
theta = 2*pi*x/Lx, z = H*y/Ly,
X = [R + a_display*|psi_interpolated|] cos(theta),
Y = [R + a_display*|psi_interpolated|] sin(theta), Z = z.
The radial displacement is a visual amplitude encoding, not physical matter
displacement. Both radial displacement and phase color must use the same
interpolated complex value. Interpolate Re(psi) and Im(psi) before deriving
amplitude or phase; interpolating hue across the -pi/+pi seam is invalid.
The cylinder surface is periodic around its circumference. The original y
periodicity remains a source-field property; attaching caps does not create
physical end conditions for that earlier model.

2. NEW VOLUMETRIC ELECTROMAGNETIC MODEL

Cylindrical coordinates (r,phi,z), with right-handed basis (e_r,e_phi,e_z),
are used. The reduced model assumes d/dphi = 0 and keeps only
E = E_phi e_phi and H = H_r e_r + H_z e_z.
The real fields describe the m=0 axisymmetric polarization, not a linearly
polarized plane wave. Rotating the r-z solution around the axis yields a
three-dimensional axisymmetric field; azimuthal perturbations are excluded.

In static, linear, isotropic material with epsilon > 0, mu > 0, sigma >= 0,
the governing equations are

epsilon * dE_phi/dt = dH_r/dz - dH_z/dr - sigma E_phi + F_phi,
mu * dH_r/dt       = +dE_phi/dz,
mu * dH_z/dt       = -(1/r) d(r E_phi)/dr.

F_phi is an imposed source term in the Ampere equation; if expressed as an
impressed current density J_ext, F_phi = -J_ext. It is not matter-created
current. The passive conduction current is J_phi = sigma E_phi.
B = mu H, D = epsilon E. E has units V/m, H A/m, B T, J A/m^2,
epsilon F/m, mu H/m, sigma S/m, distance m and physical time s.

This is a restricted electromagnetic material model. It does not solve
magnetohydrodynamics, particle kinetics, fluid flow, relativistic gravity,
plasma dispersion, nonlinear polarization, ionization, dielectric breakdown,
material motion, deformation, radiation cooling, or a black-hole interior.
The cylinder is a computational geometry, not a claim about spacetime shape.

3. AXIS REGULARITY, MATERIAL, AND BOUNDARIES

Smooth axisymmetric fields require E_phi = O(r), H_r = O(r), while H_z is
finite and even in r. Thus E_phi and H_r vanish on the axis. In particular,
lim[r->0] (1/r) d(r E_phi)/dr = 2*dE_phi/dr at r=0.
Replacing the cylindrical curl by a Cartesian derivative loses this factor.

The computational outer boundaries impose zero tangential electric field
(perfect electric conductor, PEC). They reflect pulses. A finite damping
layer may remove energy before a pulse reaches these boundaries; it is a
sponge, not a perfectly matched layer (PML) and not guaranteed reflectionless.
The axis is a regularity boundary, not a PEC wall.

The material consists of a cylindrical shell, a far-end bottom and an
optional receiving-end cover. If the outer shell radius is R_c and its
thickness is w, the initially open cavity radius is R_i = R_c - w.
The cover control C covers a fraction C of that initially open disk,
leaving clear radius R_i*sqrt(1-C), 0 <= C <= 1. Boundary-cell material
fractions use exact annular-volume intersections, with sigma_effective =
fraction*sigma_material. This is a homogenized finite-cell approximation,
not a conformal electromagnetic interface treatment.

The intake aperture denotes a geometric opening in the generic absorbing
material. Closing the cover changes the absorber mask; it does not imply
that a black-hole horizon can be opened or covered. Constant conductivity
is a coarse Ohmic absorption law and is not a measured model for aluminum,
iron, an astrophysical plasma, or any named commercial can. A good metal
reflects much of incident radiation and has a skin depth that can be much
smaller than the mesh. Larger conductivity does not guarantee larger intake.

4. ENERGY, INTAKE, AND HEATING

The actual instantaneous Poynting vector is
S_r = E_phi H_z, S_z = -E_phi H_r, S_phi = 0 [W/m^2].
The configured source is at z=0.035 m, with the mouth at z=0.10 m; the
receiving opening's outward normal is -e_z. Positive S_z is therefore
intake into this cylinder. A mouth-up drawing uses display height L-z and
does not reverse the physical source or Poynting vector. At any opening
with the opposite outward normal +e_z, negative S_z would be intake.
Signed net intake, inward positive-only throughput and reflected/outward
throughput must be distinguished. Integrating instantaneous positive-only
flux can count repeated recrossings; it is not absorptance.

Field energy is U_EM = integral[(epsilon E_phi^2 + mu(H_r^2+H_z^2))/2] dV,
with dV = 2*pi*r dr dz. Material heat is obtained from
dQ_material/dt = integral[sigma_material E_phi^2] dV >= 0.
The imposed source power is P_source = integral[F_phi E_phi] dV,
which may be signed because the source can also extract field energy.
The boundary/damping loss is kept in a distinct ledger Q_boundary.

For insulated, stationary material with constant volumetric heat capacity
C_v = rho_mass*c_p, local thermal rise is dT/dt = sigma_material E_phi^2/C_v.
An electric cell with fraction f of matter has capacity f*C_v*cell_volume.
The exported deposited density is per full electric-cell volume, so the
material temperature diagnostic uses deltaT = deposited_density/(f*C_v)
for f>0, and zero rise for empty cells. Summing f*C_v*deltaT*cell_volume
therefore recovers the total material heating energy.
This simple heating model does not include heat conduction or feedback of
temperature on sigma, epsilon, mu or phase state.

For PEC outer walls the closed-domain balance is
U_EM(t) + Q_material(t) + Q_boundary(t)
  = U_EM(0) + W_source(t) + numerical_residual(t).
Boundary loss is artificial and must never be labeled material absorption.
A balance residual measures numerical consistency; it does not validate
the constitutive material law or astronomical interpretation.

5. DISCRETIZATION UNDER REVIEW

Electric samples occupy radial/axial cell centers. H_r occupies axial faces,
and H_z radial faces including r=0. Electric-cell volume is exactly
pi*(r_outer^2-r_inner^2)*dz. Magnetic components use their matching dual
annular volumes and half-thickness end cells.

The magnetic curl K uses finite-volume cylindrical circulation. The electric
curl is its negative adjoint with respect to the volume/material weights.
This pairing, rather than ordinary Cartesian averaging, gives the correct
semidiscrete energy exchange. At the axis, the circulation stencil for the
first H_z disk is -4*E_phi(first center)/dr, consistent with E_phi~r.

Implicit midpoint time stepping uses midpoint fields for source work and
Ohmic heating. For a converged linear solve, the discrete quadratic energy
balance follows from multiplying both field updates by their midpoint
values. A small ledger residual is expected up to linear-solve and floating
point tolerances. Time step still needs refinement to resolve propagation,
oscillation and absorption even though midpoint is not explicitly CFL-limited.
In particular, midpoint is not L-stable: the isolated Ohmic decay factor
approaches -1 for sigma*dt/epsilon >> 1. Stable fields and a closed energy
ledger are insufficient if material relaxation is under-resolved. The chosen
step must also resolve epsilon/sigma; any residual stiffness should be
reported rather than disguised as physical attenuation. The implementation
caps max(sigma_effective/epsilon + sponge_rate)*dt at 0.2 in addition to
wave-propagation, carrier-period and envelope-resolution accuracy bounds.

6. INDEPENDENT ANALYTIC CHECK

In homogeneous lossless material, a PEC cylinder of radius R and length L
admits the following smooth axisymmetric mode:
alpha = j_(1,1)/R, beta = pi/L,
omega = sqrt[(alpha^2+beta^2)/(epsilon*mu)],
E_phi = A J1(alpha*r) sin(beta*z) cos(omega*t),
H_r   = A beta/(mu*omega) J1(alpha*r) cos(beta*z) sin(omega*t),
H_z   = -A alpha/(mu*omega) J0(alpha*r) sin(beta*z) sin(omega*t).
Here j_(1,1)=3.8317059702075125 is the first positive zero of J1.
These formulas were independently derived by substitution in the three
curl equations, using dJ0(x)/dx=-J1(x) and d[x J1(x)]/dx=x J0(x).
They enforce both axis regularity and zero tangential electric field at
r=R,z=0,z=L. Error against this continuum solution must decrease as the
mesh and time step are refined. Agreement checks signs, cylindrical geometry
and propagation independently of the engine's bookkeeping.

For a uniform conductivity sigma, the same spatial mode has
gamma = sigma/epsilon, Omega = sqrt(omega^2-gamma^2/4),
f(t) = exp(-gamma*t/2)[cos(Omega*t)-gamma*sin(Omega*t)/(2*Omega)],
q(t) = exp(-gamma*t/2)*sin(Omega*t)/Omega.
Replace the electric cos(omega*t) factor by f(t), and each magnetic
sin(omega*t)/omega factor by q(t). This follows from
f' = -gamma*f - omega^2*q, q'=f, f(0)=1,q(0)=0, and tests material damping
against a separate continuum solution rather than the engine's energy ledger.

Ohmic loss check: with a curl-free local electric degree of freedom and
uniform sigma, exact decay is E(t)=E(0)*exp(-sigma*t/epsilon), and deposited
heat equals the loss in epsilon*E^2/2. For the midpoint discretization the
one-step decay factor is (1-sigma*dt/(2*epsilon))/(1+sigma*dt/(2*epsilon));
the midpoint heat is exactly the quadratic energy decrease. A full-domain
lossy-mode experiment must still include electric/magnetic energy exchange.

7. VALIDATION CLAIMS AND FUTURE WORK

Required verification includes zero-field invariance, nonnegative material
heat, source/heat/boundary energy balance, analytic mode refinement,
divergence-of-B preservation with a compatible cylindrical operator,
finite/regular fields at the axis, SI normalization, and distinct source,
intake and absorption diagnostics. Engine-to-render sampling must preserve
the computed field values rather than animate invented pulse positions.

Passing those checks verifies the implemented reduced equations and mapping
at tested resolutions. It does not establish research accuracy for arbitrary
parameters, full 3D dynamics, observational validation, black-hole/wormhole
equivalence, material-specific prediction, or NASA endorsement.

To progress beyond this demonstrator: demonstrate independent code agreement,
publish systematic grid/time convergence, quantify sponge reflection, use
validated dispersive constitutive parameters, resolve material skin depth,
include additional azimuthal modes as needed, and compare a specified
measured experiment with uncertainty before making prediction claims.

INDEPENDENT CHECK RESULTS

tests/independent-em-validation.cjs currently contains ten checks. The
vacuum mode was evolved to omega*t=0.9 at 16x32,32x64,64x128 cells; its
relative volume/material-weighted field L2 errors were 0.00341559,
0.000856670,0.000214342. Uniform Ohmic sigma=0.01 S/m errors at the same
resolutions were 0.00344990,0.000865258,0.000216490. Approximately fourfold
error reduction per doubled resolution supports second-order convergence
for these smooth test cases. It does not establish convergence at material
interfaces, source discontinuities, unresolved skin depths or all settings.
Additional checks independently integrate dual volumes, inspect axis-factor
two, div(curl) cancellation, heat/temperature units, shell/cover volume,
damping-resolution bounds, Poynting signs and zero-field invariance.
