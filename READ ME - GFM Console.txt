GFM BLACK HOLE POSTULATION CONSOLE / JA21 2.0.0

OPEN
Double-click GfmJa21.exe or Start GFM Console.cmd in this folder.
Prefer D:\Codex\GFM-Blackhole-Console and keep the entire folder together.
The native JA21 window contains the combined instruments through standard
WPF WebView2. No port selection, localhost server, PowerShell script or
separate browser window is required for normal use.

Requires Windows x64, .NET Framework WPF, Microsoft Edge WebView2 Evergreen
Runtime, and a writable installation folder. Startup verifies the native
runtime and application bytes. If Windows blocks an application, its policy
remains in effect; this package does not alter or bypass that policy.

INSTRUMENTS
01 Pulse / phase / cover: actual Maxwell-Drude and thermal fields. Initial
   covered-plasma example computes two nanoseconds, then pauses. Use Run,
   Step, individual layer controls, the Poynting-flow display and JSON export.
02 Plasma / material texture: live periodic MHD, neutral complex order field,
   GF(9) samples and a separate approximate iron enthalpy laboratory.
03 Aperture / aggie: shared-spin, threshold-opening and cover postulates;
   adjustable controls and the scripted two-branch example.
04 Kerr / observations: actual adaptive null-geodesic calculations and the
   published EHT M87 amplitude comparison with every prediction/residual.
05 Equations / source audit: applied 38-finding audit and corrected GR
   calculators, with references kept separate from pulse evolution.
06 GFM transform terminal: original finite-field tools, historical equation
   registries, image equations and full source dispositions. The inherited
   narrow text-column formatting is corrected at console presentation time.

One calculation family is mounted at a time. Switching to another family
starts its repeatable initial condition. Switching among views of the same
family retains its calculated state. Export a recording before leaving an
experiment if its current history is needed. The instrument bar shows the
scope of each model. Model registry & evidence opens their detailed records.

REFRESH
Rendering and numerical integration have separate clocks. Browser animation
frames pace drawing to the available display. Calculation workers use bounded
scheduling and frame delivery, so heavy solvers cannot pile up refresh work.
GPU buffers are reused where possible. The pulse solver keeps its physical
advance interval and CFL control; the phase solver keeps its numerical rules.
Measured rates describe current hardware/load, not a guaranteed frame rate.
Kerr rays are recalculated when their physical inputs change; rotating the
camera does not rerun the geodesic solver. The EHT report is a frozen reference,
not a time-dependent simulation. Detailed actual benchmarks accompany release
verification, and per-instrument telemetry shows current throughput.

SCOPE
EM carrier phase and neutral complex order-parameter phase are different
quantities. Local MHD, iron heating, stationary Kerr, and aperture rules are
independent studies, not silently coupled equations. Cover heating does not
establish wormhole traversability. The EHT thin-ring/Gaussian references failed
the held-out comparison; that failure does not validate an alternative model.
This research prototype has no NASA qualification or full GRMHD solver.

PRESERVATION
The exact original blank console and supplied phase repository ZIPs are under
provenance/. Original deep reference paths use reversible aliases indexed in
provenance/console-source.json. Full models, licenses, raw observations, audits,
examples and historical reports remain included. Current combined verification
is validation/combined-release.json; older reports describe their own releases.
The native verification launchers record new runs under workspace/verification.
